FORSHA 7 is twenty people from Bali Crystal College, class of 2026, across two hospitality disciplines: Front Office and Housekeeping.

FORSHA stands for Front Office, Respect, Solidarity, Housekeeping, Achievement. Seven is the class. Together it reads less like an acronym and more like a way of working.

Different responsibilities. Different strengths. One team that chooses to move in the same direction.FORSHA 7

This page is a keepsake. Every name, role and portrait is real: thirty-six photographs, eighteen faces, no stock images and no invented numbers.

We rise together.

Twenty names, one identity. This is the class of 2026, and this is where we keep it.
